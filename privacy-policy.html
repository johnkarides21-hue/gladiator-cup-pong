Privacy Policy - Gladiator Cup Pong

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Gladiator Cup Pong ("the game") is a cup pong game. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

Guest accounts: When you play as a guest, you choose a display name. We store that name, a randomly generated player ID, and your game progress (coins, glory rank, shop items, avatar, and match results) on our game server so leaderboards work.

Google sign-in (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and display name from Google. We do not receive or store your password.

Device: The game does not request any Android permissions beyond internet access. It uses the device's vibration motor for haptic feedback when you throw and sink a ball. We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs.

How we use information

We use this information only to run the game: saving your progress, showing leaderboards, and matching online players. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your display name and rank appear on public leaderboards and to other players you play against. We do not share your information with third parties except hosting providers that run the game server.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the display name they choose. If you believe a child has provided more information than this, contact us and we will delete it.
