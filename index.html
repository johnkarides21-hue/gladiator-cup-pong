Swipe up from the ball to throw: a longer, faster swipe throws farther, and swiping a little left or right aims. 2v2: you and a CPU partner vs two CPUs. Each team shoots two balls a turn, and if both go in it's a BRING-BACK and you shoot again. Once per game: Loner (call a cup standing alone) and Re-rack (when the CPU is at 5 or fewer cups). Squid is a defense button (once per game): tap it while the CPU is lining up its shot to ink your cups, which makes the shooter about 75% less accurate for that one shot. Three in a row sets you On Fire (the CPU can catch fire too). If the first shooter clears the last cup, the other side gets a rebuttal shot.
Play vs the CPU, 2 players on one phone, or an online room (create a room and share the code; no accounts needed). Tap the mode button to choose.
